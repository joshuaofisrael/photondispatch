IonQ CEO DeMasi tells Barron’s the firm is building a Western quantum ecosystem and cites DARPA’s final stage

By PhotonDispatch Staff · Published 7 November 2026 · Last updated 7 November 2026

Short answer: IonQ wrote on X on 6 November 2026 that CEO Niccolo DeMasi told Barron's the company is building a quantum ecosystem for the Western world — for the United States, the Five Eyes, and NATO — and that IonQ has advanced to the final stage of DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative.

IonQ's post quotes DeMasi and adds that the company supplies systems, chips, and atomic clocks to roughly a dozen other quantum firms. A related earnings follow-up again cites progress to Stage C of DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative. Those are company claims.

How to read the message

This is executive positioning after IonQ's already-covered Q3 results, not a fresh earnings print. DARPA stage language and the "NVIDIA of quantum" metaphor come from IonQ's own communications and the Barron's interview it promotes. Independent confirmation of program stage should come from DARPA or primary contracting documents when available.

Photon Dispatch treats the 6 November X post as the public quote sheet for DeMasi's ecosystem framing and the company's DARPA-stage assertion.

Note: Figures and roadmaps are from company announcements. This is not investment advice.
