Q-CTRL raises $290 million Series C led by Salesforce Ventures, valued above $1 billion

By PhotonDispatch Staff

Published 14 November 2026 · Last updated 14 November 2026

Short answer: Quantum software company Q-CTRL has raised US$290 million (A$415 million) in a Series C round led by Salesforce Ventures, the company announced on Thursday. Q-CTRL says the round was oversubscribed and expanded twice, and calls it the largest quantum-sector fundraise for a software-focused business.

Quartz, citing Bloomberg, reports that the round values the company at more than $1 billion. Bullhound Capital and Affinity Partners made significant contributions, Q-CTRL says, and SiliconANGLE lists AMD Ventures among more than a dozen other investors. Quartz names returning investors including In-Q-Tel, Lockheed Martin Ventures, DCVC and Square Peg.

## What Q-CTRL sells

The company's best-known product is Ironstone Opal, a navigation system meant to work when GPS is jammed or unavailable. SiliconANGLE explains that it uses quantum magnetometers and gravimeters to read small variations in the Earth's magnetic field and gravity, then matches them against pre-loaded maps. Q-CTRL says the system is being deployed on commercial aircraft and autonomous vehicles; Quartz reports work with Airbus and Lockheed Martin.

Q-CTRL also sells software that calibrates qubits and reduces error rates on quantum computers, which it describes as "AI for quantum" infrastructure.

## What the money is for

Q-CTRL says it will use the funds to scale its software and expand global deployment of its quantum navigation systems. The claims about performance in the field are the company's own; we have not seen independent test data.
