Abu Dhabi Maritime Academy partners with Quincus and Quantinuum on quantum research

By PhotonDispatch Staff · Published 9 November 2026 · Last updated 9 November 2026

Abu Dhabi Maritime Academy (ADMA), part of AD Ports Group, has announced a research partnership with Quincus and Quantinuum to explore quantum computing, artificial intelligence and high-performance computing for maritime operations, transport logistics and mining, The Quantum Insider reported on November 9. The announcement was made at Quantum Innovation Summit 2026 in Dubai.

The report describes a research collaboration, not a finished commercial product or a measured quantum speedup.

What the partners say they will do

According to The Quantum Insider, the partners will start by identifying operational challenges and then assess where advanced computing could improve processes across the three sectors. That is a narrower commitment than announcing a working optimisation product. The report does not name a specific algorithm, dataset, quantum processor or performance target for the work.

Earlier maritime quantum work

The academy previously signed a memorandum of understanding with Vernewell Group and launched the Blue Initiative in October 2025. That earlier programme covers communications, sensing and optimisation alongside education and industry engagement. The new collaboration adds named technology partners. The Quantum Insider does not disclose a budget, project timetable or research milestones.

What is still missing

A meaningful assessment will need concrete use cases and comparisons with established classical methods. That distinction matters for optimisation, where classical software already handles many industrial problems. A later study might show quantum techniques helping in a defined setting; it would not automatically imply broad superiority.

For now, the November 9 announcement establishes the collaboration and its intended research areas. It does not establish that a quantum computer has improved shipping schedules, mining operations or transport costs.
