IonQ posts record Q3 revenue of $173.8 million and raises 2026 guidance to $505 to $515 million

By PhotonDispatch Staff · Published 6 November 2026 · Last updated 6 November 2026

Short answer: IonQ said third quarter 2026 GAAP revenue was $173.8 million, up 336% from a year earlier, and raised full year 2026 revenue guidance to between $505 million and $515 million as remaining performance obligations rose to $1.2 billion. Figures below are taken only from the company's 5 November 2026 investor release and the matching X post.

Headline results from the IR release

IonQ called the quarter its sixth consecutive record and the strongest in company history. GAAP revenue of $173.8 million compared with $39.9 million in the year ago quarter on the face of the release's statements of operations. The IonQ Platform segment contributed $90.3 million, up 127%, which the company tied to global Tempo quantum computer deployments. SkyWater Foundry Services contributed $83.5 million.

Net income attributable to IonQ was $564.0 million, or $1.42 of GAAP EPS, reflecting a large non cash mark to market impact from warrants. Adjusted EBITDA loss was $133.6 million and Adjusted EPS was a loss of $0.29; those adjusted figures are IonQ's non GAAP measures as defined in the release.

Cash, cash equivalents, and investments were $1.7 billion as of 30 September 2026, and $3.7 billion on a pro forma basis including a $2.0 billion equity offering that closed on 16 October 2026. Remaining performance obligations more than doubled to $1.2 billion, the company said.

Guidance and other IR bullets

For full year 2026, IonQ raised revenue guidance to $505 million to $515 million and said it expects 110% year over year growth in quantum computing revenue. Other items listed in the release include advancing as the only trapped ion company to Stage C of DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative, with a potential programme value of up to $300 million; plans for a Superion 256 system as the first on premise quantum computer at NVIDIA's Accelerated Quantum Research Center; and an agreement to acquire Elisity to extend the quantum security platform.

How Photon Dispatch reads it

This is a booked revenue and guidance update from a public company, not a new qubit fidelity claim. Readers should separate the GAAP profit, which IonQ itself links heavily to warrant accounting, from the still large adjusted operating loss. Contract backlog at $1.2 billion is the commercial demand signal IonQ chose to stress alongside the guidance raise.
