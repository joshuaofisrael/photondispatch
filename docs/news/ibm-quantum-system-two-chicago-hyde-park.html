IBM unveils Quantum System Two in Chicago at Hyde Park Labs

By PhotonDispatch Staff · Published 7 November 2026 · Last updated 7 November 2026

Short answer: IBM unveiled an IBM Quantum System Two in Chicago on 6 November 2026, calling it the company’s first quantum computer in Illinois and its most advanced system available, hosted at the University of Chicago’s Hyde Park Labs and powered by IBM Quantum Nighthawk.

Where it sits, and where it goes next

IBM’s newsroom release says the machine is initially located at Hyde Park Labs and will later move to the Illinois Quantum and Microelectronics Park (IQMP) as the anchor system of the National Quantum Algorithm Center (NQAC). Governor JB Pritzker, IBM chairman and CEO Arvind Krishna, IQMP CEO Harley Johnson and University of Chicago president Paul Alivisatos are quoted marking the public unveiling.

Krishna said the deployment can deliver scientifically useful results to organisations across Illinois and beyond. Those are company and state remarks at a ribbon-cutting, not independent performance benchmarks published in the release.

Who is already working through NQAC

Since NQAC’s founding, IBM says industry partners have been building applications alongside its researchers. The release names Deloitte and AbbVie, working with IBM and Northwestern on quantum molecular simulation for drug discovery, and EPRI with the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign on quantum optimisation for power grids. Fermilab is cited for high-energy physics and distributed quantum computing work. IBM also references a recent University of Chicago demonstration of quantum advantage over leading classical methods with guaranteed correctness; that demonstration is mentioned, not reproduced, here.

Workforce angle

Earlier this year IBM said it would establish the FutureNow Chicago centre at IQMP with City Colleges of Chicago, aiming to bring 750 jobs across AI, cybersecurity, data science and quantum. The System Two unveiling is presented as both a research tool and a workforce signal for the state’s quantum cluster.

For Photon Dispatch readers, the concrete news is hardware on the ground in Chicago: a Nighthawk-powered System Two now operational at Hyde Park Labs, with a planned move into IQMP once the park campus is ready.
