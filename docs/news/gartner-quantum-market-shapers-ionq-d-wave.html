IonQ and D-Wave say Gartner named them Market Shapers in its first quantum computing quadrant

By PhotonDispatch Staff · Published 12 November 2026 · Last updated 12 November 2026

Short answer: Two listed quantum companies, IonQ and D-Wave, say research firm Gartner has positioned them as Market Shapers in its first Emerging Market Quadrant for Quantum Computing, Established Vendors. The Gartner report itself is paid; the placements below are as the companies describe them.

IonQ announced its placement on 10 November. In its press release, the company quoted Gartner as describing Market Shapers as vendors that have proven they can reliably deliver and support advanced systems in commercial settings. IonQ cited the report as authored by Gaurav Gupta, Matthew Brisse and Chirag Dekate, and repeated the announcement on X on 11 November, dating the report 4 November 2026.

D-Wave followed on 12 November. Its release, filed with the SEC as an exhibit to a Form 8-K, says it was recognised as a Market Shaper as of November 2026 and was among six vendors in the upper right quadrant. D-Wave quoted the report as saying Market Shapers are moving beyond research experiments toward scalable fault-tolerant architectures, high-fidelity logical qubits and enterprise software stacks. The company also posted the news on X.

Neither release lists the other four vendors in the upper right quadrant, and PhotonDispatch has not seen the full report. Both companies reproduce Gartner's standard disclaimer that it does not endorse any vendor and that its research reflects the opinions of its analysts rather than statements of fact.

Note: This report summarises company announcements and a filing; figures and characterisations are the companies' own and have not been independently verified. This is not investment advice.
