UK's National Quantum Computing Centre signs Phasecraft software licence for SparQ users

By PhotonDispatch Staff · Published 8 November 2026 · Last updated 8 November 2026

The UK's National Quantum Computing Centre (NQCC) has signed an enterprise licence agreement with quantum algorithms company Phasecraft, giving UK organisations access to Phasecraft's software through the NQCC's SparQ Access initiative, Phasecraft announced on 6 November. Applications open in late November 2026 and the first cohort starts in spring 2027.

First software on SparQ

Simon Plant, deputy director at the NQCC, said the deal "marks the first time we are making quantum software available through our SparQ programme." The centre has until now focused on giving UK users access to quantum hardware.

Three tools

Participants will work with one of three Phasecraft tools. Magritte is for materials and molecular modelling, covering resource estimation and quantum circuit construction. Monet is a quantum-enhanced density functional theory tool for modelling materials and molecules. Mondrian is a quantum-enhanced optimisation platform for problems in areas such as finance, logistics or infrastructure, available through a web interface or API.

Who can apply

The NQCC and Phasecraft will invite proposals from UK businesses and researchers with challenges in materials science, chemistry and optimisation, with support from Phasecraft's scientists and engineers. Places are also reserved for the NQCC and academics doing non-commercial research.

Access to the software does not by itself show that quantum methods beat classical ones for a given problem. Results will depend on the problems chosen and the hardware used.
