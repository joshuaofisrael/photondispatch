11 November 2026

IBM said Nighthawk r3 will be built to run 10,000 quantum operations across 520 programmable qubits, with a plan to link two processors into a 1,040-qubit system in 2027.

9 November 2026

IBM's "Quantum Queue Optimization for Error Mitigation" application describes a scheduler that groups compatible jobs on one chip and reuses stored noise models to cut repeated error-mitigation work.

5 November 2026

IBM, Oak Ridge’s Quantum Science Center and AMD unveiled a co-design platform on 5 Nov 2026 pairing IBM’s 120-qubit Nighthawk with ORNL’s Umbra HPC system (AMD Instinct MI355X GPUs and 5th Gen EPYC CPUs) for quantum error-correction research aimed at DOE’s 2028 goals.
